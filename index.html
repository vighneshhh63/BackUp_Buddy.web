Step-by-Step Backup Guides

Choose your device or platform and follow the simple checklist.

Open your trusted photo backup service, enable automatic backup, choose the account/storage location, and verify that recent photos appear online.
Turn on contact synchronization with your main account and periodically confirm that contacts are visible from another device or web account.
Open the app's Chat Backup settings, choose a backup frequency, include important media if needed, and check the date of the latest successful backup.
Create a clearly named backup folder containing your important work, certificates, projects and personal documents. Keep another copy in a separate location.
Keep source code in version control, avoid relying only on a local Unity project, and back up large assets/builds separately when required.
Copy your most important folders to an external drive. Safely eject it after the backup and keep it disconnected when not in use.
Periodically save important photos, videos and memories that exist only on social platforms. Keep them in your normal backup system.
Use a strong unique password, enable two-factor authentication, and keep recovery details accessible and up to date.
Remove old or unnecessary third-party access so your account and stored information have fewer points of failure.
